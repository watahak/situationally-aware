## Structural Persistence & Correction Mechanics

Cap-weighted index reflexivity is self-reinforcing on both sides. Passive inflows inflate the bubble (US mega-cap) while forced rebalancing deepens the depression (international, China tech). Both engines are non-fundamental — they persist until a breaker fires. This is why Z-scores stay extreme longer than mean-reversion models predict.

### Depression-Side Correction

**1. Cash Flow Buyback Trap.** Suppressed price spikes FCF yield; buybacks shrink float faster than institutions sell. *Active — Chinese tech running aggressive buybacks at elevated yields.*

**2. Seller Inventory Depletion.** Institutional allocation approaching zero means no supply left to sell. *Approaching — foreign China allocation at multi-decade lows.*

**3. Capital Ownership Transfer.** Domestic/non-aligned capital buys the yield gap Western mandates refuse. *Active — Southbound flows consistently positive.*

**4. Arbitrage Prisoner's Dilemma.** Extreme earnings yield spread (US ~2.8% vs China ~10%) incentivizes defection from consensus. *Building — 700bps spread at KWEB/QQQ 1.6th pct, no visible defection.*

**5. Permanent only if** — expropriation, nationalization, or asset freeze. Otherwise, cash flow = gravity.

### Bubble-Side Breakers

**1. Symmetrical Outflow Cascade.** Net redemptions force selling proportional to index weight — Mag 7 at ~30% absorb ~30% of all selling. *Dormant — net flows positive, CTA 78th pct.*

**2. Earnings Yield vs Risk-Free Squeeze.** FCF yield (0.88%) already below Treasuries (~4.5%). *Active but overpowered by passive flow volume.*

**3. AI Capex ROI Disconnect.** $250B/yr capex must generate proportional returns or FCF shrinks. $75B intercompany revenue is circular. *Lagging — capex accelerating, ROI mixed, 4–8Q lag.*

**4. Corporate Supply Expansion.** SBC, issuance, insider selling dilute passive inflows. *Moderate — SBC elevated, insider selling periodic.*

**5. Index Caps / Regulation.** Special rebalances at concentration thresholds; antitrust at GDP-gap extremes. *Slow-moving — +37pp gap, rebalances occurred.*

4. **Watch mechanical gauges, not headlines.** CTA percentile (78th), dealer gamma sign ($3.2B/1%), yield spread (0.88% vs ~4.5%), net passive flow direction.

**In practice**: Stay in the flow, stay undersized, and have a pre-defined response for when CTA hits 95th percentile or gamma flips negative.
